Privacy
Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This policy explains how Dental Health (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use this website, call us, or visit the practice at 6000 Boulevard East, West New York, NJ 07093.

This website is a static site: we do not run analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or third-party cookies. The fonts are loaded from a third-party service and the site itself stores no personal data.

01

Information you give us

We collect the details you choose to share when you call us, write to us, or send the contact form (name, email, phone, and message). This information is used only to respond to your enquiry and to arrange appointments.

02

Health information

Medical and dental history is collected only in person at the practice, kept secure and confidential, and never shared with third parties except where required by law or with your written consent.

03

Cookies & analytics

This site does not set marketing cookies. The Tailwind CSS framework is loaded from a content delivery network; no personal data is transmitted in the process.

04

Sharing

We never sell or rent your information. Information is shared only with your explicit permission, or where we are legally required to do so.

05

Your rights

You may at any time ask us what information we hold about you, request a copy, ask for corrections, or ask for it to be deleted — contact us and we will act within 30 days.

06

Contact

For any privacy question or request, call (201) 555-0192, or write to the address above.
